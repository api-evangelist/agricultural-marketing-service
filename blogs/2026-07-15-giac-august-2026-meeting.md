GIAC August 2026 Meeting dparker Wed, 07/15/2026 - 13:49 The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Grain Inspection Advisory Committee meeting will be held August 25-26, 2026, in Kansas City, Missouri. The agenda for the upcoming meeting will focus on grain inspection matters including, but not limited to, regulatory updates, technology modernization initiatives, inspection policies, and operational updates.
