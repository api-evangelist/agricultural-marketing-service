USDA Announces Availability of New Fertilizer Transportation Data dparker Mon, 08/10/2026 - 11:16 Date August 10, 2026 The U.S. Department of Agriculture’s (USDA) Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS) today announced the launch of a new enhancement to the Fertilizer Transportation Dashboard on its Agricultural Transportation Open Data Platform (AgTransport) . The enhancement adds anhydrous ammonia pipeline rates (measured in dollars per ton) to complement existing fertilizer data.
