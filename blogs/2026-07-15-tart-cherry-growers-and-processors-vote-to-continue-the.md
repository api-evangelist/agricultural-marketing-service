Tart Cherry Growers and Processors Vote to Continue the Federal Marketing Order dparker Wed, 07/15/2026 - 13:07 Date July 15, 2026 The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) today announced that tart cherry growers and processors voted to continue their marketing order program. In a referendum held March 30 through April 20, 2026, 62.7% of voting tart cherry growers and 69.6% of voting tart cherry processors favor continuance of the marketing order program, representing 75.3% of the production volume and 65.6% of the processed volume voted in the referendum, respectively.
