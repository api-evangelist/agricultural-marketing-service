USDA Announces $2.3 Million Awarded through the Micro-Grants for Food Security Program DARIUS J. PARKER Fri, 09/25/2026 - 12:13 Date September 25, 2026 The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) today announced approximately $2.3 million in grant funding awarded to departments of agriculture in Hawaii, Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, Guam, and American Samoa through the Mico-Grants for Food Security Program (MGFSP).
