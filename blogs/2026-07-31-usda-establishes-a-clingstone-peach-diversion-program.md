USDA Establishes a Clingstone Peach Diversion Program sblack Fri, 07/31/2026 - 08:33 Date July 31, 2026 Providing Economic Relief to Farmers Amidst Changing Market Conditions The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) announced today a Clingstone Peach Diversion Program to provide reimbursements to producers for the voluntary removal of peach trees. The program will further USDA’s goal to put farmers first by addressing industry needs amid changing market conditions.
