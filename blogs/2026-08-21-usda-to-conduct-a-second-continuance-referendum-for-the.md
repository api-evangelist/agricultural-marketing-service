USDA to Conduct a Second Continuance Referendum for the Christmas Tree Research and Promotion Program dparker Fri, 08/21/2026 - 13:43 Date August 21, 2026 The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) today announced it will conduct a second referendum among eligible Christmas tree domestic producers and importers to decide whether to continue their research and promotion order. Dates will be announced after consultation with the Real Christmas Tree Board.
