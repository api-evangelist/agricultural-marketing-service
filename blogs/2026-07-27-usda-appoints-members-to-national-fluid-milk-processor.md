USDA Appoints Members to National Fluid Milk Processor Promotion Board egarman Mon, 07/27/2026 - 08:21 Date July 27, 2026 The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) today announced the appointment of six members to serve on the National Fluid Milk Processor Promotion Board. The six members will serve three-year terms, effective immediately through June 30, 2029.
