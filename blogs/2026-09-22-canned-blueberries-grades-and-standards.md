Canned Blueberries Grades and Standards dparker Tue, 09/22/2026 - 12:11 Grades of Canned Blueberries U.S. Grade A or U.S. Fancy is the quality of canned blueberries that possess similar varietal characteristics; that possess a very good color; that are practically free from defects; that possess a good character; that possess a normal flavor and odor; and that score not less than 90 points when scored in accordance with the scoring system outlined in this subpart.
